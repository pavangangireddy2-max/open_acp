SDE Learning Systems Career Framework

Career progression from Associate to Lead across 21 skill areas. Last updated March 2026.

What makes this role different: You create content, design curriculum, and engineer the delivery. You need domain expertise (FullStack, DS/ML, GenAI), curriculum design skills, and platform orchestration abilities. You design learning experiences, then engineer them by selecting and configuring the right systems (code playgrounds, evaluation engines, AI tutors, cloud IDEs). You bridge three areas most roles keep separate: pedagogy, technical systems, and content creation. The product is the learning system itself, not standalone software.

Compensation and Scope

Example: "Associate SDE, Learning Systems - FullStack" or "Associate SDE, Learning Systems - GenAI". Creates content with guidance (lessons, exercises, projects, assessments). Learns to integrate code playgrounds, evaluation judges, and learning platforms. Handles 0.5-1 domain, 40-100 content hours, reaches 2K-5K students.
SDE 1, Learning Systems - [Domain]
9L to 16L | 1-3+ years
Example: "SDE 1, Learning Systems - DS/ML". Creates domain content independently (lessons, exercises, projects). Engineers learning systems by selecting appropriate platforms (IDEs, cloud environments, assessment engines). Owns 1-2 domains. Manages 100-250 hours, reaches 5K-10K students.
SDE 2, Learning Systems - [Domain Portfolio]
18L to 24L | 3+ years
Example: "SDE 2, Learning Systems - FullStack, System Design & CS Core". Leads content creation across multiple domains. Architects platform systems and optimizes evaluation pipelines. Manages 2-5 team members across 3-4 domains. Manages 250-450 hours, reaches 10K-20K students.
SDE Lead, Learning Systems - [Domain Portfolio]
24L to 30L | 5+ years
Example: "SDE Lead, Learning Systems - Programming, FullStack, CS Core, DevOps". Owns content strategy and creation across portfolio. Designs platform architecture and GenAI-driven content systems. Leads 15-25 members across 4-6 domains. Manages 450+ hours, reaches 20K-40K+ students.
SDE 3, Learning Systems - [Domain Portfolio]
30L+ | 7+ years
Example: "SDE 3, Learning Systems - Portfolio (All Domains)". Sets org-wide content and curriculum strategy. Owns EdTech infrastructure evolution, GenAI content pipelines, and cross-product technical roadmap.
Reality check: Domain complexity affects how many domains you can handle. FullStack and GenAI are 2x harder than English or Aptitude. System Design and DS/ML are 2.5x harder. Don't assume linear scaling.

Performance Rating Framework (SDE Lead Level)

4 pillars, weighted. Target is 3.0+ on 5-point scale.

Pillar 1: Performance (50%)

  • Impact on Learning Outcomes (40%): Assessment score improvements, competency progression
  • Feedback Resolution (20%): Issues resolved within SLA, quality of implementation
  • Industry Upgrades (20%): Planned updates on schedule, emerging skills coverage
  • Pedagogy/R&D (20%): Concept understanding improvements, completion rates, delivery innovation

Pillar 2: Role Competence (25%)

  • Curriculum Quality (40%): Pedagogical excellence, content coherence, engagement quality
  • GenAI Orchestration & Automation (20%): Effective use of AI for content production, quality of automation systems, cost/velocity optimization through AI, team GenAI capability development
  • Cost Efficiency (15%): Cost per learning hour, cost per assessment, resource optimization
  • Content Velocity (15%): Monthly content hours delivery, practice/assessment production
  • Cross-functional Collaboration (10%): Request fulfillment within timeframes, stakeholder satisfaction
GenAI Orchestration (20% of Role Competence = 5% of total rating): An SDE Lead who fails to build effective GenAI systems will score poorly here even if other areas are strong. This measures both personal AI usage discipline and ability to enable team-wide AI capability. Without this, content velocity and cost efficiency targets become unrealistic.

Pillar 3: Develop the Best (15%)

  • Power Performers Created (30%): Members ready for next level, training program design
  • Timely Feedback (20%): Regular 1-on-1s, actionable feedback, development plan clarity
  • Hiring (15%): Active participation, candidate evaluation quality, new hire success rate
  • Talent Nurturing (20%): High potential recognition, development opportunities, succession planning
  • Growth Opportunities (15%): Stretch assignments, cross-functional exposure, leadership development

Pillar 4: Culture and Values (10%)

  • Framework Adherence (20%): Following org processes, execution consistency
  • Learning Culture (20%): Conducting learning hours, knowledge sharing, skill development
  • Outcome Over Output (20%): Business impact focus vs. volume, quality over quantity
  • Growth Mindset (20%): Staying current with trends, learning from new tech, embracing change
  • Mission Alignment (20%): Living company values, brand protection, mission-driven decisions

Rating Scale

Rating Label Meaning
5 Exceptional Exceeded significantly
4 Exceeds Above expectations
3 Meets As expected (target)
2 Needs Improvement Struggled to meet
1 Unsatisfactory Failed to meet

Calculation: Final Rating = (Performance × 0.50) + (Competence × 0.25) + (Develop Best × 0.15) + (Culture × 0.10)

Target is 3.0+. Below 3.0 means performance issues. Above 4.0 should be rare.
