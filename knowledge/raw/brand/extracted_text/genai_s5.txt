Important Considerations


    1.   Polling Trigger: The "Watch Rows" module in Google Sheets is a polling
         trigger, meaning it checks for new data at the intervals you've set. It
         doesn't respond instantly to changes in the sheet.

    2.   Manual Execution: If you want to test your scenario or run it immediately,
         you can use the "Run once" button. However, this won't affect the
         scheduled execution; it's only for manual testing.
