Max-Heap


             A priority queue is a type of queue where elements are served
             based on priority rather than on the order in which they arrive.



                      0       1      2      3       4          5         6

                     VIP   Regular   VIP   VIP    Regular   Regular   Regular




                                So, VIP are prioritized ﬁrst,
                           irrespective of the order of insertion.
Priority Queue

Max-Heap


                 It is a special type of tree-based data structure where the largest value is
                                    always at the top (root node of tree).



                                                 Example

             ➢      50 is the largest element.
             ➢      Removing the top (50) moves the next greater element (30) to the top.

Key Takeaways



         Queue (FIFO - First In, First Out): Elements are added at
         the rear and removed from the front, making it useful
         for sequential processing.


         Priority Queue: A special queue where elements are
         served based on priority rather than insertion order.
         Implemented as Max-Heap (largest element at the top)
         or Min-Heap (smallest element at the top).
Priority Queue

Key Takeaways

     Operations:

      ➢    Queue: push(), pop(), front(), back(), size(), empty().
      ➢    Priority Queue: push(), pop(), top(), size(), empty().


     Examples:

      ➢    Queue: Used for task scheduling and managing
           requests in order.
      ➢    Priority Queue: Used for designing emergency rooms
           management system (patients with severe conditions
           treated ﬁrst).

Priority Queue

Emergency Room


         In an emergency room management system, patients are

         prioritized based on the severity of their condition using a

         priority queue. This ensures that patients with the most critical

         conditions receive immediate attention and treatment, while

         those with less severe cases are handled subsequently.


         Each patient is represented by their name and severity level,

         where the severity level determines their priority in the queue.
Priority Queue

Emergency Room

    Supported Operations:


         1.   Add Patient: A(severity, name)

                 ✓   Adds a new patient with the speciﬁed severity and name to the queue.

         2.   Treat and Remove Patient: T()

                 ✓   Displays and removes the patient with the highest severity from the queue.

         3.   Display Next Patient: D()

                 ✓   Displays the patient with the highest severity without removing them from the queue.



                      No two patients will have the same severity or name at the same time.
Emergency Room

Example

                               Input                             Output

                              5
                              A 5 John
                                                                   Alice
                              A 10 Alice
                              T                                     Bob
                              A 7 Bob
                              D


           ➢     A 5 John : Adds patient John with severity 5 to the queue.
           ➢     A 10 Alice : Adds patient Alice with severity 10 to the queue.
           ➢     T : Removes and treats the patient with the highest severity which is Alice.
           ➢     A 7 Bob : Adds patient Bob with severity 7 to the queue.
           ➢     D : Displays the next patient to be treated, which is Bob.
