Operation Costs




              Adding at the End (push_back/append):

              ➢    On average, 1 step to add the element.

              Removing from the End (pop_back):

              ➢    Steps: 1 step to remove the element.
Functions

Operation Costs


        Inserting in the Middle (insert):

            ➢   Scenario: An element is inserted somewhere other than the end.

            ➢   Steps: Approximately N steps are needed (where N is the number of elements), since
                every element after the insertion point must be moved one position over.

        Deleting from the Middle (erase):

            ➢   Steps: Approximately N steps (where N is the number of elements), as each element
                following the removed element must be shifted to close the gap.
