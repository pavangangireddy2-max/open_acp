AI Engineer Ladder

Career framework for content-department domain teams · v2, August 2026 · titles follow the AI Engineer – [Domain] Learning Systems pattern · descriptors carried verbatim from the March 2026 SDE Learning Systems framework · editable master: role_cards.xlsx (6 tabs)
What changed in v2: titles renamed to the AI Engineer pattern; every progression area and rating line is wired to named KPI rows — anything in a mono chip is a live row on the KPI tracker or the team view, so reviews read off the sheets instead of impressions. Areas with no chip say so by design. Level content, comp bands and the rating weights are unchanged from the source framework.

The ladder

Five levels, linear — AI Engineer 3 sits above Lead (30L+ vs 24–30L, org-wide scope); the progression matrix below deliberately stops at Lead. Comp bands included; strip this column before wide sharing if needed.
Associate AI Engineer – [Domain] Learning Systems
formerly Associate SDE, Learning Systems - [Domain Name]
4.5L to 7L | 0-1 years
Example: "Associate AI Engineer – FullStack Learning Systems" or "Associate AI Engineer – GenAI Learning Systems". Creates content with guidance (lessons, exercises, projects, assessments). Learns to integrate code playgrounds, evaluation judges, and learning platforms. Handles 0.5-1 domain, 40-100 content hours, reaches 2K-5K students.
Answers for: Contributes to this team's Section B rows under review — no rows answered for yet; growth is read through review evidence.
AI Engineer 1 – [Domain] Learning Systems
formerly SDE 1, Learning Systems - [Domain]
9L to 16L | 1-3+ years
Example: "AI Engineer 1 – DS/ML Learning Systems". Creates domain content independently (lessons, exercises, projects). Engineers learning systems by selecting appropriate platforms (IDEs, cloud environments, assessment engines). Owns 1-2 domains. Manages 100-250 hours, reaches 5K-10K students.
Answers for: Answers for the Section B velocity + quality rows of the modules they own (their content hours, pieces, issue-recurrence share).
AI Engineer 2 – [Domain Portfolio] Learning Systems
formerly SDE 2, Learning Systems - [Domain Portfolio]
18L to 24L | 3+ years
Example: "AI Engineer 2 – FullStack, System Design & CS Core Learning Systems". Leads content creation across multiple domains. Architects platform systems and optimizes evaluation pipelines. Manages 2-5 team members across 3-4 domains. Manages 250-450 hours, reaches 10K-20K students.
Answers for: Answers for a domain slice of Section B — effectiveness, velocity and Evaluation Environment Coverage for their courses; first Section C asks raised in their name.
AI Engineer Lead – [Domain Portfolio] Learning Systems
formerly SDE Lead, Learning Systems - [Domain Portfolio]
24L to 30L | 5+ years
Example: "AI Engineer Lead – Programming, FullStack, CS Core, DevOps Learning Systems". Owns content strategy and creation across portfolio. Designs platform architecture and GenAI-driven content systems. Leads 15-25 members across 4-6 domains. Manages 450+ hours, reaches 20K-40K+ students.
Answers for: Answers for the team's full Section B at review and supports the Section A lane numbers; the rating framework below applies as written.
AI Engineer 3 – [Domain Portfolio] Learning Systems
formerly SDE 3, Learning Systems - [Domain Portfolio]
30L+ | 7+ years
Example: "AI Engineer 3 – Portfolio (All Domains) Learning Systems". Sets org-wide content and curriculum strategy. Owns EdTech infrastructure evolution, GenAI content pipelines, and cross-product technical roadmap.
Answers for: Shapes org-tracker rows and cross-domain standards; portfolio spans teams — reads through §5 department KPIs and org KRAs, not one team view.

Project Manager — the operating role

Not a sixth ladder level — a parallel operating seat, one per domain team, added August 2026 with the Team Ops & People metric block. The maker levels above own the content and the systems; the PM runs the team’s operating rhythm.
