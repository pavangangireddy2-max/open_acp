11. Reference: Learning Platform linkage — the student journey from class to SPI

Sub-KPIs diagnose; lane KPIs answer — when a number here breaks, the review question goes to the owning lane's KPI, not to the sub-KPI. Each step now carries a named owner (column below) — who runs the step day-to-day; accountability for the rolled-up number stays with lane KPIs. External owners (Program Ops, Instructors dept, Mentors) are governed through the §7 asks: a step red 2 consecutive weeks escalates HOD-to-HOD. Lever targets are product metrics (samples below, baselines to be set).
