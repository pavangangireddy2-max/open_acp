NIAT Org KPIs as KRAs — Department KPI Tracker

Training sheet and FY 2026-27 tracker, merged · August 2026 · one row per trackable KPI, grouped under the org KRA it moves · training columns fold out · editable workbook: kra_training_sheet.xlsx (3 tabs)
How to read this sheet: a KRA (Key Result Area) is an organizational outcome NIAT is judged on — it carries the target; each KRA block keeps its color family (a reading aid — identity is always in the text). Each numbered row is one trackable KPI — per-batch rows split into B3 / B4, band metrics decompose into one row per band. Variance = Budgeted − Actual (org Head-Abstract convention): negative = beat on higher-is-better rows; rows flagged lower is better read inverted. Blank Budgeted = first-quarter baseline sets it. The three training columns — Dependent metrics (the trigger metrics that compose or precede the KPI), the Funnel (causal chain to the org KPI), and Functions that move it (the real teams, per the HOD one-pager key terms; Lane stays the accountability owner, not a team) — are collapsed by default: toggle them here, expand the column group in the workbook. Markers: ORG org-owned scoreboard, tracked not owned · CROSS-DEPT resolved across departments, we route · SAMPLE illustrative until baselines land · CASCADE same KPI feeds two KRAs.
