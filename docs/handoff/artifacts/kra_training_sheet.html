NIAT Org KPIs as KRAs — Department KPI Map

Training sheet · August 2026 · one row per department KPI, grouped under the org KRA it moves · editable workbook: kra_training_sheet.xlsx
How to read this sheet: a KRA (Key Result Area) is an organizational outcome NIAT is judged on — it carries the target. Each KRA block carries its own color family (a reading aid — identity is always in the text). Lane (KPI owner) is an accountability grouping of KPIs, not a team — the lane lead answers for the number at review. Functions that move it are the real teams whose day job moves the KPI (Product, Engineering, Content SMEs, Pedagogy, DA/DE, CSI team) — training runs per function, and §2 is the room-by-room cut. Dependent metrics are the trigger metrics that compose or precede a KPI — the journey-wise numbers you watch to move the headline; the Funnel is the causal chain to the org KPI. Markers: ORG org-owned scoreboard, tracked not owned · CROSS-DEPT resolved across departments, we route · SAMPLE illustrative until baselines land · CASCADE same KPI feeds two KRAs.
