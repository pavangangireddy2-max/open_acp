# Stage: Difficulty Balancing — Final Course Quiz

## Your Role
You are calibrating the difficulty of a comprehensive final course quiz.

## What's Different from Skill Assessment
- Hardest quiz in the course — difficulty ceiling is higher
- Must still be fair (no impossible questions)
- Broader range of difficulty needed (easy recall to hard synthesis)
- Expected score distribution: wider spread than module quizzes

## Process
Follow `skill_assessment/04_difficulty_balancing` with these modifications:

### Difficulty Targets
- Easy: 15-20% (4-5 questions: baseline recall, confidence builders)
- Medium: 40-45% (10-12 questions: solid application)
- Hard: 25-30% (7-8 questions: analysis, cross-module integration)
- Very Hard: 10-15% (2-3 questions: synthesis, evaluation — differentiators)

### Score Distribution Expectations
- Top 10%: 80-95%
- Above average: 65-80%
- Average: 50-65%
- Below average: 35-50%
- The quiz should differentiate across the full range

### Ordering Strategy
- Start with 3-4 easy questions (build confidence)
- Middle section: mixed medium and hard
- End with 2-3 very hard (synthesis/evaluation)
- Don't cluster all hard questions together

### Time Verification
- Easy: 1-2 minutes each
- Medium: 2-3 minutes each
- Hard: 3-5 minutes each
- Very Hard: 5-8 minutes each
- Total: 60-75 minutes

## Output Format
Same as `skill_assessment/04_difficulty_balancing`, with very_hard tier added.

## Quality Criteria
- Difficulty spans easy to very hard
- Quiz differentiates across the full student ability range
- Time fits within 60-75 minutes
- Starts with confidence builders, ends with differentiators
- Passable at 50-55% without getting the hardest questions right
