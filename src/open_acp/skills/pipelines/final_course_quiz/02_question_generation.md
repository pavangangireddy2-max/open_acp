# Stage: Question Generation — Final Course Quiz

## Your Role
You are writing 25-30 comprehensive questions that evaluate full-course mastery.

## What's Different from Fortnight Quiz
- Much larger scope (entire course, not 2 modules)
- Higher Bloom levels (Analyze/Evaluate, not just Apply)
- "Big idea" synthesis questions that test holistic understanding
- More diverse question formats

## Process
Follow `fortnight_quiz/02_question_generation` with these modifications:

### Format Mix
- 12-15 MCQ questions (higher difficulty than module quizzes)
- 4-5 Short answer / explanation (3-5 sentences each)
- 3-4 Code-based (tracing, debugging, or "what's wrong with this design?")
- 3-4 Scenario/case study (multi-part, testing integration)
- 2-3 "Big idea" / synthesis (open-ended, testing holistic understanding)

### "Big Idea" Questions
These are the signature of a final quiz:
- "Compare three approaches to [problem type] learned in Modules 2, 4, and 6. When would you use each?"
- "Given this real-world scenario, design a solution using concepts from at least 3 modules. Justify your choices."
- These test whether the student can synthesize, not just recall

### Scenario/Case Study Questions
Multi-part questions around a realistic scenario:
- Part A: Identify the problem (Analyze)
- Part B: Propose a solution (Apply)
- Part C: Justify your choice over alternatives (Evaluate)

### Difficulty
- This is the hardest quiz in the course
- Expected: top students score 80-90%, average students 55-65%
- Include 3-5 truly challenging questions (Analyze/Evaluate)

## Output Format
Same as `fortnight_quiz/02_question_generation`, with additional multi-part questions.

## Quality Criteria
- 25-30 questions covering all modules
- At least 2 "big idea" synthesis questions
- At least 3 scenario/case study questions
- At least 5 Analyze/Evaluate level questions
- Every module represented
- Questions test understanding, not memorization
