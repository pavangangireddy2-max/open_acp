# Stage: Learning Objectives — Summary Cheatsheet

## Your Role
You are generating objectives for a quick-reference cheatsheet. These objectives are about RECALL and LOOKUP, not deep learning.

## What's Different from Other Pipelines
- Bloom levels are **Remember and Understand** only
- The cheatsheet is a reference tool — objectives describe what learners can LOOK UP and recall
- Assessment is speed: "can you find X in under 10 seconds?"

## Process
Follow `concept_explainer/02_objectives` with these modifications:

### Step 2: Write Objectives
Use recall and lookup verbs:
- **Remember**: recall, list, state, name, recognize
- **Understand**: summarize, paraphrase, describe in one sentence

Example: "Recall the syntax for Python's 5 most common string methods without looking them up"

### Step 4: Assessment
- Assessment is implicit: the cheatsheet itself IS the reference tool
- Success = learner can quickly find and apply the right syntax/concept from the cheatsheet

## Output Format
Same as `concept_explainer/02_objectives`.

## Quality Criteria
- Objectives are about recall speed and accuracy
- No objectives above Apply level (that's not what cheatsheets are for)
- Every section of the cheatsheet maps to at least one objective
- Objectives reflect real-world "I need to look this up" scenarios
