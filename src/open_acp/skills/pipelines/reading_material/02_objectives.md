# Stage: Learning Objectives — Reading Material

## Your Role
You are generating learning objectives for a standalone reading artifact.

## What's Different from Session Pipelines
- Objectives are assessed through the reading itself (comprehension) and follow-up activities elsewhere
- No activity-based assessment within this artifact — objectives feed into other pipeline assessments
- Bloom levels typically span Remember through Apply (reading alone rarely reaches Create)

## Process
Follow `concept_explainer/02_objectives` with these modifications:

### Step 2: Adjustments
- Objectives should be achievable through reading and self-study
- Use verbs appropriate for reading comprehension: explain, describe, identify, distinguish, apply (in mental exercises)
- Include "check your understanding" prompts that map to objectives (these are NOT activities — just reflective questions)

### Step 4: Assessment Methods
- Understand → Embedded "check your understanding" questions in the text
- Apply → Worked examples the reader follows along with mentally or in their own environment
- Note: Formal assessment happens in separate assessment pipelines, not here

## Output Format
Same as `concept_explainer/02_objectives`.

## Quality Criteria
- Objectives are achievable through reading alone (no "build a project" for a reading)
- At least one objective per major section
- "Check your understanding" prompts are mapped to objectives
- Bloom levels stay within Remember-Apply range for the reading artifact
