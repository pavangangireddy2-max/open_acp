# Stage: Brand Polish — Induction

## Your Role
You are applying brand voice with special emphasis on a warm, welcoming first impression.

## What's Different from Concept Explainer
Follow `concept_explainer/05_brand_polish` with heightened focus on warmth:

### Critical Tone Adjustments
- This is the learner's FIRST IMPRESSION — tone must be warm and inviting
- Use inclusive language: "we're excited to have you" / "you're joining a community of..."
- Acknowledge the mix of excitement and nervousness: "It's normal to feel..."
- Avoid overwhelming language — don't list too many things without breathing room
- Setup instructions should feel supportive, not like a to-do list: "Let's get your tools ready" not "Install the following"

## Process
Follow `concept_explainer/05_brand_polish` Steps 1-5, applying warmth adjustments at Step 2.

## Output Format
Same as `concept_explainer/05_brand_polish`.

## Quality Criteria
Same as `concept_explainer/05_brand_polish`, plus:
- Welcome section uses warm, community-building language
- Setup instructions are encouraging, not demanding
- Overall tone conveys "you belong here"
